Last updated: October 7, 2026

Overview

THIẾT KẾ MENU is a ChatGPT plugin designed to assist users with menu design, menu data review, concept development, and menu editing.

Information processed

The plugin may process information that users voluntarily provide during their interaction, such as menu text, item names, prices, images, design references, brand information, and editing instructions.

Purpose of processing

User-provided information is used only to perform the menu-design and editing tasks requested by the user.

Data isolation

The plugin is designed to work with information available in the user's current interaction. It does not include preloaded customer menus or private menu data from the developer's other ChatGPT projects.

External services

This version of the plugin does not independently operate an external database or external API for storing user menu data.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell user-provided menu data or personal information to third parties.

Security

Reasonable care is taken to avoid including private customer data or data from unrelated menu projects in the plugin itself.

Processing through ChatGPT and OpenAI

THIẾT KẾ MENU operates within ChatGPT. Content that users provide while using the plugin may therefore be processed by OpenAI as part of providing the ChatGPT service. OpenAI's handling of such data is governed by its own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data retention

The plugin does not independently maintain a database or separate persistent storage of user-provided menu data or customer data. Data processed or retained by ChatGPT or OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's applicable data retention policies and user settings.

Data deletion and privacy requests

For data stored or processed by ChatGPT or OpenAI, users should use the privacy and data controls provided by OpenAI. For privacy questions or requests specifically related to THIẾT KẾ MENU or the developer, users may contact the developer at chaupq80@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the plugin's functionality or data practices change.
